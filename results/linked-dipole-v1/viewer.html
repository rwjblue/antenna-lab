How to read this: Every pattern uses a common absolute −30 to +12 dBi scale; no curve is normalized to its own peak. Polar radius is gain minus −30 dBi, with deeper nulls clipped at the center. Vertical cuts show only the upper hemisphere; the left side uses the opposite azimuth's actual computed samples. Horizontal azimuth 0° follows the wire's +x axis to the right, increasing counterclockwise; these are antenna-relative angles, not compass bearings. Radiation into the upper hemisphere is computed by integrating absolute gain over solid angle and dividing by 4π. This is distinct from NEC's printed efficiency. Feedline/common-mode currents, mast, coax/choke/tuner losses and unmeasured hardware are absent from the baseline. Patterns cannot guarantee contact distances. Retuning requires physical adjustments.
