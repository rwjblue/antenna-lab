# Antenna Lab

## Radiation-pattern comparisons

Prefer polar plots when comparing directional radiation patterns. Overlay
comparable traces on a shared absolute dBi scale, label the gain reference,
and identify the elevation and azimuth cuts. Use Cartesian plots when they
make a specific detail easier to read.

Follow the [linked-dipole plots](docs/case-studies/linked-dipole-method.md#reading-the-polar-radiation-plots)
for an example.
